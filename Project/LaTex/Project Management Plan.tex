	\section*{Objectives}
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Social media platform geared towards CS students with the intent of learning. Measured by user satisfaction with the experience and how it affected their learning.
		\item The platform is relatively easy to use and is intuitive for new users / CS students. Measured by users on how difficult the platform was to use. 
	\end{itemize}
	
	\section*{Scope}
	
	\subsection*{Requirements:}
	
	\begin{itemize} 
		\item Connect with friends
		\item Learn from peers, or systems within the application
		\item Harmless experience free of online toxicity 
		\item Form study groups with other users
		\item Schedule activities with other users
	\end{itemize}
	
	\subsection*{Use cases:} 
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Learn and communicate all in one place
		\item Maintain contact with friends compared to other team oriented platforms 
		\item Find people with similar interests  
	\end{itemize}
